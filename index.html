About Verisavo

Verisavo is a connected intelligence layer for African markets. We help organisations understand what is happening in the market around them, why it may be happening, what remains unknown and what it could mean for the decision ahead.

The information organisations need already exists. It sits in company data, public records, digital platforms, research, field observations and the knowledge of people on the ground, but it rarely forms a coherent picture. Verisavo is being built to connect that evidence, keep each piece tied to its source, place and time, and show clearly where it falls short.

What we believe

These principles shape how the platform is designed and how we talk about what it knows.

01

Evidence before assertion

Important conclusions should rest on evidence. An answer is not true because a model produced it.

02

Context before conclusion

A data point means little without where, when, who it applies to and how it was collected.

03

Relationships matter

Prices, distribution, infrastructure, policy and behaviour shape one another. We look at how the pieces connect.

04

Unknown is a valid answer

When the evidence cannot answer a question, we say so and name the Intelligence Gap.

05

Markets change

Understanding is never final. Market Memory records what changed and which evidence changed it.

06

Human decisions stay human

Verisavo supports the decision. The people responsible for it make it.

From market reality to human decision

Each layer has its own job. Together they turn scattered observations into an understanding a team can act on.

Why Africa

Africa is not one market

Fifty-four countries, thousands of cities and local economies, many languages, regulatory systems, distribution networks and ways of buying. That diversity is why Verisavo exists, and why every answer should define the geography it applies to.

SavoScouts

Intelligence from the ground

Some market realities are poorly documented, especially in informal markets. When an Intelligence Gap could change a decision, Verisavo can gather targeted Ground-Level Intelligence through SavoScouts, its governed contributor network. Observations are designed to be geo-tagged, time-stamped and verified before they become evidence.
